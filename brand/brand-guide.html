Let your wishes take flight.

Brand guide, version 1. The logo, colours and type for IccheGhuri, the AI learning platform for students in Bangladesh. The tagline above is a placeholder suggestion, not approved copy.

01 · The idea

A wish, on a string.

Icche (ইচ্ছে) means wish and ghuri (ঘুড়ি) means kite. The logo is a paper kite caught mid-flight: something a student makes, launches and steers.

  1. 1
    Four folded panelsThe kite is folded paper, lit from the top-left. Four shades of one orange give it depth without gradients.
  2. 2
    Tilted 18°, flyingIt leans into the wind. The tilt is fixed: the logo is never straightened or rotated further.
  3. 3
    The stringA gentle S from the tail tip. Someone is holding the other end: the student stays in control.
  4. 4
    Kites over the i'sThe wordmark is custom-drawn in rounded lowercase. The dots of both i's are small kites at the same tilt.

05 · Typography

The logo is drawn. Everything else is Inter.

The wordmark is custom lettering with kites over the i's. It is never typed: always place the logo file. All other text uses Inter, a free, open-source family built for screens. Bangla text uses Noto Sans Bengali.

06 · Icons and pattern

Small sizes, big surfaces.

Below 48 px the string is dropped. At 16 px the kite uses two shades, split along its spine. For large surfaces, the small kite repeats as a pattern.
